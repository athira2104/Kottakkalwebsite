Kathakali
The Art of Story Telling

Kathakali is a Complete Theatre of Kerala, encompassing music, sculpture, dance & drama, all within a halo of supra-earthly alien gorgeous somatic decor. Extracting its kinetic spirits from ancient martial, folk and classical arts of the state, under the patronage of great maestros, ‘This Art of Story Telling’ has become a global favourite of all theatre enthusiasts, evolving through the last six centuries.
